# v0.5 — controls and mix

Logged 2026-09-24. Tag: `v0.5`. Followed [PLAN-ui.md](../PLAN-ui.md).

## What changed from v0.4

- **Four audio buses.** Engine, weather, ambience and music, between the layers and the master
  fader, with a user level each that persists. Road noise rides the engine bus; the one-shots go
  through theirs, because a thunderclap wired straight to the master would ignore the weather
  fader.
- **Weather stopped shouting.** Rain 0.55 → 0.42, wind 0.3 → 0.26, thunder 0.9 → 0.55, and the
  weather bus to 0.55. Measured against v0.4 in a worktree: the 2–8 kHz band falls 7.4 dB while
  the 20–240 Hz band stays at −49.8 dB exactly.
- **Two More menus.** The dots in the volume pill hold one fader per bus; the dots at the end of
  the row hold Reduce motion, Low quality and the keyboard shortcuts — which the *start screen*
  used to own, for no reason beyond having been written first. The row went from five controls
  to three.
- **Tooltips**, on hover after 350 ms and on keyboard focus immediately, never on a coarse
  pointer. `title` is gone from every control.
- **Rain is generated rather than filtered**, and wind gusts. See below.
- **The radio panel** gained a live tuner, a Music slider that is actually music, Saxophone Jazz
  in place of Lo-fi beats, and short-link handling.
- **One head unit per car**, mounted in the console instead of laid on the dash.

## Notes

**Rain.** The old layer was white noise through a highpass and a bandpass: rain's spectrum with
none of its structure, which is what television static is. It is now a bed plus thousands of
discrete impacts, written sample by sample from a seeded PRNG so the generator is pure and its
statistics testable. Two findings shaped it. Brightness saturates by about 450 drops per second,
because past that the texture simply *is* the impacts — so density lives in the buffer and
brightness in a lowpass, rather than both fighting over one sound. And crest factor is the
obvious measure of peakiness and the wrong one: it is a maximum, so one lucky drop moves it and
it is not monotone across seeds. The tests use the fraction of samples above 3 RMS.

**Two calibrations.** Both rewrites arrived about 6 dB hot — the rain because two decorrelated
copies sum and the normalised buffer has no trim where the old filtered chain did, the wind
because three summed bands with a brown source under the lowest are simply louder than one white
bandpass. Both were measured against the previous build and trimmed back, so the level fix above
survived the texture work.

**What the Music slider cannot do.** The Spotify embed is a cross-origin iframe; its volume is
not reachable from this code under any circumstances. The slider moves the tuner, and the panel
says where Spotify's own volume lives. Having "Music" secretly duck the world instead was
considered and rejected as a control that lies about what it does.

**Live radio.** [Radio Browser](https://api.radio-browser.info/): free, no key, and it answers
with `access-control-allow-origin: *`. The app has only a latitude and a longitude and the API
has no reverse geocoder, so one geo query does both jobs — the stations nearby, and through the
countries they report, which country to ask for the rest of the band. Most of what the API
returns will not play, and every filter is a stream that would otherwise fail silently. For
Malaysia, 44 of 53 https stations are playable and the tuner loads 41.

The stream is the one audible thing outside the mixer, because `createMediaElementSource` returns
silence for a cross-origin stream unless the server sends CORS headers and icecast servers
overwhelmingly do not.

**Spotify links.** Single songs already worked — tracks, albums, `intl-` prefixes, `?si=`
suffixes and bare URIs all parsed. What failed was `spotify.link`, the link the mobile Share
button hands you, and the fact that nothing in the UI said a song was allowed. Both were the bug.

**Preset ids are verified, not trusted.** An embed answers 200 with an error page for a dead
playlist; a live one comes back around 150 kB, a dead one about 6. That check found that
Spotify's own editorial "Saxophone Jazz" does not embed at all, and that the fourth preset had
been labelled "Night drive" while pointing at a playlist called Chill Pop.

**The radio block.** Reported mid-release as "a weird rectangle block jutting out" of every
radio, and it was three versions of one mistake: a shared faceplate at nearly twice double-DIN
size floating 5–8 cm proud of the console; a *second* fascia drawn by the roadster and the camper
at the same point, one of them canted 35°; and the hatchback's sun visors, which are roof
furniture hanging where the roof left in v0.2. Found by probing a single pixel with `gl.readPixels`
after each frame and hiding every mesh in turn — bisecting by eye had failed four times first.

## Unchanged

The three vehicles, the camper's see-through walls, the swap fade, the engine voice, weather
effects and the lighting director, the location picker, persistence and quality tiers.

## Deferred

Recorded CC0 weather loops — the seam exists and `loadOrSynth` already prefers a file; committing
binaries to a repo that advertises having none is the owner's call. Spotify OAuth for full
playback. Station search by name or genre. The scene radio's dial does not turn with the tuner:
its knobs are merged static geometry, and extracting one is a bigger change than the flourish is
worth.
