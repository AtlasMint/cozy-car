# v0.2 — the open-roof build

Logged 2026-09-23. Tag: `v0.2`.

## What changed from v0.1

- **Whole car, no roof.** Both side walls, all glass, both headlights, tail-lights and
  mirrors, sills under both doors, a full-width dash with a glovebox, whole front seats and
  bench. The roof panel is gone; the window frames and two header rails stay so the hatchback
  silhouette reads and the camera looks down into the cabin. The cut plane, caps and cut
  materials are removed from the code.
- **Green hoodie.** The driver wears `#4F8F5B` with a darker hood, chosen to sit against the
  seafoam body and brown seat fabric.
- **Pick a location.** The weather badge is a button. It opens a search backed by Open-Meteo's
  geocoding API; a pick is stored under `shotgun.location.v1` and used instead of geolocation
  until "Use my location" clears it.
- The engine-bay fill light is gone with the closed bonnet.

## Unchanged

Camera and stage, motion rig, driver idle, weather effects and lighting, audio, the radio
push-in and Spotify panel, persistence and quality tiers.

## Notes

The rain and snow shelter box still includes the roof volume, so the open cabin stays dry.
