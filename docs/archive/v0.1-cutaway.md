# v0.1 — the cutaway build

Archived 2026-09-23. Tag: `v0.1`.

## What it was

A fixed isometric diorama of a small seafoam hatchback, sliced open on its near side like a
museum cross-section model, seen from the rear-left quarter. The near doors, glass and half of
everything on the cut plane were never built; sheared parts were capped in a flat pale "cut"
material. The roof was sheared on its own staggered plane because, from that camera, a roof cut
on the main plane sat directly between the lens and the driver.

## What shipped

Everything in `docs/PLAN-car.md`, one commit per phase: isometric rig and slab, the cutaway car,
the driver and idle system, ignition and the four-band engine rig, Focus mode with the scenery
treadmill, Open-Meteo weather, the weather director (rain, snow, spray, glass, lightning,
day/night lighting), layered audio with synth fallbacks, the radio push-in and Spotify panel,
persistence, quality probe and docs.

## Why it was retired

The cut plane fought the camera and the car. It hid the driver until the roof cut was staggered,
demanded caps on every part it touched, and made the car read as a diagram rather than a toy.
v0.2 keeps the same camera and interior but builds the whole car with no roof, so the cabin is
seen from above with nothing sliced.

## What carries forward

Everything except the cut: the stage, camera, motion, driver, weather, audio, interaction and UI
are unchanged. The rain exclusion box still includes the roof volume, so the open cabin stays dry.
