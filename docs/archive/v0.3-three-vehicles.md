# v0.3 — three vehicles

Logged 2026-09-23. Tag: `v0.3`.

## What changed from v0.2

- **Three vehicles instead of one.** A hatchback (unchanged), a 5.6 m camper van and a yellow
  roadster, chosen from a picker in the bottom-right cluster or by cycling with **V**. The
  choice is remembered, and `#vehicle=` overrides it.
- **A vehicle is a spec.** `src/core/vehicles.ts` holds everything another system reads:
  dimensions, cabin, anchors, camera framing, shelter box, motion profile, engine voice and
  driver pose. Bodies are one file each under `src/scene/car/bodies/`, exporting one `build()`.
  The hatchback's numbers moved verbatim, and a geometry fingerprint proved every extraction
  phase changed nothing on screen.
- **A new engine.** The old two detuned sawtooths through an rpm-swept lowpass could not sound
  like an engine. It is now a combustion pulse train through fixed resonators: one oscillator
  at `rpm / 120` carrying a per-vehicle order table, so harmonic *k* is engine order *k/2* and
  the half-orders that make an idle lumpy come free. Load is finally separate from rpm, so
  Chill and Focus differ in timbre rather than only pitch.
- **Swapping.** About 1.1 s, camera-led, with the vehicle exchanged mid-move and the crank
  suppressed. Built vehicles are cached hidden for the session, so every swap after the first
  is recompile-free.
- **Performance.** The exhaust became one instanced draw call instead of up to fourteen sprite
  materials, flat interior detail stopped casting shadows, and bodies gained a detail group the
  low-quality tier hides. The hatchback went from 123 to 117 draw calls.
- **The world** grew backwards to −12 and the streetlight rhythm was evened, because the van's
  wider frame showed the plinth's rear edge and exposed a latent strobe.

## Unchanged

Camera angles, the treadmill, weather effects and the lighting director, the radio push-in and
Spotify panel, the location picker, persistence and quality tiers, and the driver — one figure
and one green hoodie for all three vehicles, with only its pose per vehicle.

## Notes

**No cutaways.** v0.2's rule still holds: the roof is the only thing missing. The van's wall top
is *derived* rather than chosen — a point in the cabin clears the near wall only when
`y + z + wallZ > wallTopY`, and solving that for the hatchback's ~30% floor exposure gives
1.95 m. That is what makes it a high-top van rather than a coachbuilt box, and why it needs no
shear to be readable from this camera.

**One shelter test for all three.** The exclusion box moved onto the spec, but the view-ray test
that implements it is unchanged, so there is no shader branch and no recompile on a swap.

**Two rules the architecture now depends on.** No vehicle may add a material key, and the
point-light count is fixed at boot. Either would recompile every lit program in the scene on
every swap.

**Palettes are measured, not picked.** Every body has to separate from the plinth it stands on.
A bright lemon sports car sits at about 1.05:1 against the slab and dissolves into it, and a
cream camper cannot separate from a warm neutral slab flanked by white road markings at any
lightness — hence the deep yellow and the dusty blue.
