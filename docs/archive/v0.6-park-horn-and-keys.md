# v0.6 — park, horn and keys

In progress. Logged so far 2026-09-28; not yet tagged. What is below shipped against the first
three GitHub issues. The rest of v0.6 is Park mode — planned in [PLAN-park.md](../PLAN-park.md),
to be logged here when it lands, and then tagged.

## What changed from v0.5

- **Listen says what it is doing.** Opening a stream takes as long as somebody else's server
  takes, and the button used to show nothing for that whole time — a slow station looked like a
  press that had not worked. It has three states now: Listen, then *Tuning…* with a ring
  turning until you can actually hear something, then Stop. "Hear something" is the audio
  element's own `playing` event, not `play()` resolving, which only means the request was
  accepted. Pressing it again mid-tune gives up on the stream, and a station that neither plays
  nor errors is abandoned at `RADIO.CONNECT_TIMEOUT_MS`.
- **One keyboard.** Every key goes through a registry
  ([src/ui/hotkeys.ts](../../src/ui/hotkeys.ts)) that holds the guards once, and the shortcut
  list in the settings menu is that registry read back. New: **M** mutes, **R** opens and closes
  the radio, **H** is the horn. A binding registered without a label stays off the list, which is
  deliberate.
- **A horn on every vehicle.** Hold **H**, or press the middle of the steering wheel; holding
  holds it and a click is a tap. Two tones roughly a minor third apart, a sawtooth pair through a
  peak at the flare's mode, per vehicle: 400/500 Hz on the hatchback, 440/550 on the roadster,
  310/390 on the camper.

## Notes

**Where the loading state could have stuck.** Two paths would have made the new state
permanent. An error raised while connecting was ignored, because the handler only ran while
`playing`; a stream that failed on the way up would have left the dial turning for good. And
`stalled` shared that handler, which would have skipped past any station that merely took a
moment to fill its first buffer. They are separate handlers now: an error counts in both states,
a stall only once something was playing, and while connecting the timeout is the judge.

**The keyboard registry.** Three controls each brought their own `keydown` listener and their own
copy of the same three guards — no modifiers, not while typing, never the space bar belonging to
a focused button — and the shortcut sentence in the settings menu was a fourth thing to keep in
step by hand. Hoisting the guards had one consequence worth recording: `preventDefault` on the
space bar moved with them, which would have stopped space scrolling an open popover on a vehicle
with nothing bound to it. `onDown` may return `false` to decline a press, and that restored it.
The list is drawn each time the menu opens, because the menu is built before most of the keys
are bound.

**The horn's voice.** A car horn is a steel diaphragm driven past its own resonance by a contact
breaker: it chops the air rather than swinging it. So the tone is a sawtooth through a peaking
filter on the second harmonic — a pair of sines is a doorbell — with a little bandpassed air
over it, and both ends droop in pitch as the diaphragm reaches speed and loses it. A press whose
release never arrives (a lost keyup, a window that lost focus mid-press) ends on its own at
`HORN_MIX.maxHoldS`.

**Measured.** At the master tap with the volume at its default, a held horn first came out at
0.92 on the heaviest vehicle — level with the ceiling the camper reaches in Focus under thunder,
which the headroom note in `AUDIO` says nothing new may raise. Equal gain is not equal loudness
an octave down: a lower sawtooth pair puts far more energy where the ear is sensitive. The level
and the per-vehicle trims were set by measurement and every vehicle now lands between 0.56 and
0.67, limiter untouched.

**The press point costs the bodies nothing.** Every spec already says where the steering wheel
is and how big it is, in the numbers the driver's grips are built from, so the hitbox is placed
from those and works on a vehicle whose wheel is not a wheel at all. A test holds it clear of the
radio's hitbox on every spec, because two boxes that overlap make one of them unreachable in
whichever order the ray happens to hit them.

## Unchanged

The three vehicles and their bodies, the swap fade, the engine voice, weather and the lighting
director, the location picker, persistence and quality tiers, Spotify and the tuner's band.

## Deferred

The scene radio still does not turn its dial with the tuner. A horn light or a visible press on
the wheel — the horn is sound only. Park mode, which is the rest of this version: see
[PLAN-park.md](../PLAN-park.md).
